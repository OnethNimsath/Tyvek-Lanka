About Tyvek Lanka

Crafting premium wristbands that bring people together and make every event unforgettable

Our Story

Founded with a passion for bringing people together, Tyvek Lanka has been at the forefront of creating high-quality wristbands for parties, events, and celebrations across Sri Lanka. What started as a small venture has grown into a trusted name in the event industry.

We believe that every event deserves the perfect wristband - whether it's a music festival, corporate gathering, or private party. Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction drives everything we do.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide exceptional wristband solutions that enhance event experiences, foster connections, and create lasting memories for our customers across Sri Lanka and beyond.

Our Vision

To become the leading wristband provider in South Asia, known for innovation, quality, and exceptional customer service that brings communities together.

Why Choose Tyvek Lanka?

Premium Quality

We use only the finest Tyvek materials to ensure durability, comfort, and style in every wristband.

Custom Designs

From colors to logos, we offer complete customization to match your event’s unique branding and theme.

Fast Delivery

Quick turnaround times and reliable delivery across Sri Lanka to ensure your events run smoothly.
